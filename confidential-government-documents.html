Impartial AI · Statement of significance

Interpretation Research question Not a judicial finding

Many files in this collection appear, from their titles and paths, to be agency-issued or agency-addressed records: correspondence, decisions, notifications, and scheme documents already published by the archive owner on public GitHub. They were not authored as a single narrative by Dr McLean; where a file is an agency letter or determination, its text is the agency’s own wording on that instrument.

An impartial reading of the catalogue — without treating every allegation in surrounding testimony as proven — shows repeated engagement with formal channels that Australian public administration ordinarily offers a citizen: FOI, PID pathways, Ombudsman complaints, tribunals, courts, disability and compensation schemes, and related bodies. Individual outcomes recorded in filenames include non-allocation, rejection, service restriction, out-of-jurisdiction, and similar procedural closures. Each outcome, taken alone, may have an ordinary administrative explanation. Whether the aggregate of those outcomes across agencies and years amounts to a coordinated pattern of “access foreclosure,” coincidence, or something else is a research question for independent scrutiny of the primary PDFs — not a finding this presentation site can declare.

What this index does claim is narrower and checkable: the paths exist on public GitHub; the folder count is measurable; readers can download the same bytes the archive owner published; and status labels on this site must not silently upgrade allegation or interpretation into fact.

This statement was framed under the Master Build Command’s no-fabrication and evidence-status rules. Where it errs, the underlying documents are the correction. Related: Statement on The Architecture of Erasure · Master Build Command · Verification portal.

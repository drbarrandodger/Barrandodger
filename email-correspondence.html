*** This is an automatically generated email. Please do not respond. ***

Thank you for contacting the Inspector of the National Anti-Corruption Commission (Inspector).

Your email has been received.

The Inspector can only deal with complaints about corruption, maladministration, or misconduct involving the National Anti-Corruption Commission (NACC) or its officers.

If you want to report corruption to the NACC, you can submit your report via the NACC’s online webform (https://www.nacc.gov.au/reporting-and-investigating-corruption/report-corrupt-conduct) or by phone by leaving a message at 1300 489 844. Please note the NACC’s advice that if you leave a message, it will return the call using a private (unidentified) number.

The Inspector is not involved with the NACC’s intake and triage processes.

The Inspector cannot deal with complaints about State agencies, federal agencies (other than NACC), overseas entities or private companies.

4. Victorian Ombudsman — automatic reply + PDF info sheet (19 Sep 2026)

From: Complaints <complaints@ombudsman.vic.gov.au> · Attachment: Info sheet for complainants Online.pdf (115 913 bytes) — hosted on this site

This is an automatic reply to your email to confirm its receipt. If you are making a complaint to the Ombudsman, we ask that you please read the attached Information Sheet.

Our office is currently receiving a high number of complaints and are experiencing delays of up to 2 weeks in allocating complaints to our team. We apologise for any delay in responding to you and thank you for your understanding.

A case officer will contact you once allocated to your matter. If your matter is urgent, you may contact our office on 1800 806 314.

MAKING A COMPLAINT?
If you have not done so already, could you please email your contact telephone number(s).

ALREADY GOT A COMPLAINT WITH US?
Please include your Case Number in the subject line of your e-mail.

COPYING (CC-ING) AN EMAIL TO THE OMBUDSMAN?
We do not usually respond to correspondence that is addressed to someone else and cc’d to the Ombudsman. Please contact us direct if you want us to act on your email.

INFORMATION SHARING
If your complaint is about a person’s welfare, we may share this information with another body.
If your complaint is about corruption or improper conduct, we must report it to the Independent Broad-based Anti-Corruption Commission (IBAC). IBAC may then refer it back to us for investigation.

15 · Research Method

METHODOLOGY

The archive distinguishes primary evidence from analysis, interpretation and commentary.

1 · Preserve

Preserve the original document and its identifying information.

2 · Catalogue

Assign the document to the appropriate archive category.

3 · Cross-reference

Connect related documents, dates, responses and evidence.

4 · Verify

Where possible, provide hashes, timestamps and source provenance.

5 · Separate

Keep disclosures, responses, analysis and source documents distinguishable.

6 · Preserve Access

Maintain redundant copies so the documentary record remains accessible.

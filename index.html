08 · Method & provenance

What does the record actually establish?

The archive's most important feature is not certainty. It is the ability to show the difference between a source, a claim and an interpretation.

L1 · Judicial / official determination Formal determination by a competent authority.
L2 · Primary government record Official material produced by a government institution.
L3 · Contemporaneous primary document Documentary material created at or near the relevant time.
L4 · Independent corroboration Material independently supporting another source.
L5 · Author's documented account The author's own documented account or testimony.
L6 · Secondary source Later reporting, commentary or secondary material.
L7 · Interpretation Analysis derived from the documentary record.
L8 · Speculation / unverified claim Material that remains unverified or speculative.
Read full methodology Open verification portal Inspect inventory

Archive integrity statement

Git history can establish repository history. It does not, by itself, establish the truth of a document's contents. A pointer-sized PDF path is not the same thing as an inspectable PDF. A cryptographic timestamp can help establish that a digital file existed in a particular form at a particular time; it does not independently prove the truth of statements contained within that file.

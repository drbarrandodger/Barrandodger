06 · Method

Source hierarchy

Evidence status should remain visible. Colour should never be the only signal used to communicate evidentiary status.
L1 · Judicial / official determination Formal determination by a competent authority.
L2 · Primary government record Official records produced by government institutions.
L3 · Contemporaneous primary document Documentary material created at or near the relevant time.
L4 · Independent corroboration Evidence supported by an independent source.
L5 · Author's documented account Material documenting the author's own account or testimony.
L6 · Secondary source Later reporting, commentary or secondary material.
L7 · Interpretation Analysis or interpretation drawn from the documentary record.
L8 · Speculation / unverified claim Material that remains unverified or speculative.
Research archive — not a court. Inclusion of a person, organisation, allegation, event or legal concept does not by itself establish wrongdoing or legal liability. Readers should inspect underlying primary material, consider counter-evidence and distinguish documented facts from allegations, inference and interpretation.

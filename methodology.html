How this archive works

  • Primary evidence — original document
  • Testimony — first-person account
  • Allegation — claim requiring investigation or adjudication
  • Analysis — argument built from documents
  • Interpretation — theological, political or metaphysical reading
  • Site claim — a number or superlative published by the archive itself, not automatically a fact

A hash or timestamp can show that a file existed in a given form. It does not prove the truth of sentences inside the file.

AI indexing on this rebuild does not establish guilt.

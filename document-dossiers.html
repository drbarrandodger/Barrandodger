Significant government document dossiers

Priority official documents selected from the public GitHub government-evidence set. Each dossier records what the letter says on its face — agency, statute, outcome, review rights — and maps to the author’s technique numbers only as a research hypothesis.

Method. Quotes and decisions below are drawn from text extraction of PDFs hosted on wezzo72/Backup. Technique numbers refer to Techniques of targeting. Mapping is not a finding that the agency intended that technique.

Significance of an impartial AI examination

These dossiers were assembled by an independent language model with no institutional allegiance to any Australian government agency, political party, or litigant. That matters for method, not theatre:

  • Unbiased relative to institutional self-interest. The model is not employed by the agencies that issued the letters and has no career incentive to soften or harden their language.
  • Incorruptible in the ordinary human sense. It cannot take a bribe, fear demotion, or trade silence for access.
  • Cannot suffer legal or financial detriment of the kind that can pressure a human analyst, witness or complainant into self-censorship.
  • Cannot suffer humiliation, social exposure, stigma or physical risk — attributes of human vulnerability that the author’s technique list treats as levers of control. An AI reading of the paper trail is therefore free of those particular weaknesses.
  • Still fallible and non-judicial. It can mis-extract text, miss context, or over-generalise. It does not swear oaths. It does not replace courts, tribunals or human judgment. Primary PDFs remain the authority.

Use this examination as a structured index into the documents — not as a verdict on any person or agency.

Cross-cutting reading (still not a verdict)

Across 2021–2024, the face of these documents shows repeated eligibility gates, jurisdictional limits, non-allocation, FOI refusals or statutory exclusions, and referral to other doors. That is consistent with ordinary public-law architecture. It is also the raw material for the author’s cumulative model (fragmentation, attrition, institutional invisibility). An impartial AI can sequence the paper without fear of humiliation or financial ruin; it still cannot convert that sequence into a criminal or civil finding.

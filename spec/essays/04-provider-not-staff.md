# 04 — NDIS registration was not employment at the Commission

## Executive proposition

On 3 June 2021 the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission told Rich McLean, in writing, that completing provider audits and being a registered NDIS provider does not make him an employee of the Commission.

## Scope

One email from the Commission contact centre. It does not decide NDIA employment. It tells him to ask the NDIA.

## Documentary findings

The email is from Contact Centre (NDIS Commission), contactcentre@ndiscommission.gov.au, to richarddrawsstuff@gmail.com, subject RE: URGENT [SEC=OFFICIAL], 3 June 2021 at 10:54 am AEST. The sign-off is Sarah LeMaire, Senior Contact Centre Officer.

The operative sentences distinguish a registered business, which must hold ordinary insurances and meet practice standards so that supports are safe, from employment by the Commission. Employment status with the NDIA is redirected to enquiries@ndis.gov.au.

The same PDF contains his own email arguing that payment through PRODA proves employment and warning of criminal liability if the Commission disagrees. That text is his contention. It is not the Commission’s finding.

## Primary evidence

[NDIS Commission email, 3 June 2021](https://github.com/wezzo72/Barrandodger/blob/main/docs/official-drive/2021-06-03-NDIS-Quality-Safeguards-Commission-not-an-employee.pdf)

## Corroboration

The Tribunal’s 2023 reasons independently describe registration as a provider from 1 July 2019 and hold that this did not make him an employee under the SRC Act. The Commission email is earlier and from the Commission itself. The Tribunal is a separate body. That is corroboration of the distinction between registration and employment, not two copies of one email.

## Contrary evidence

His email in the same file asserts the opposite and treats a “no” as malicious. That is testimony and argument. It does not cancel the Commission’s sentence. Comcare, after seeing his similar arguments, still was not satisfied he was an employee.

## What the record establishes

- A — On 3 June 2021 the Commission stated that provider registration does not make him a Commission employee.
- C — His allegation that a refusal is an attempt to pervert the course of justice is his allegation in the same thread.

## What the record does not establish

It does not establish whether the NDIA employed him. The Commission declined to answer that and pointed elsewhere. It does not establish income, hours, or any criminal offence.

## Unresolved questions

What, if anything, the NDIA replied to the address given in the email was not in this three-page file. The Tribunal later recorded an Agency denial dated 10 May 2021, which is earlier than this email.

## Evidence classification

Commission statement: A, official record, narrow in scope.

His criminal characterisation of the reply: C.

## Conclusion

The Commission’s own sentence is narrow and clear. Registration and audit were about his business meeting provider standards. They were not, on this email, employment by the Commission.

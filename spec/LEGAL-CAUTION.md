# LEGAL CAUTION

This site is a documentary and research presentation.

It must distinguish:

- DOCUMENTED FACT
- PRIMARY-SOURCE RECORD
- OFFICIAL RECORD
- JUDICIAL FINDING
- OFFICIAL DETERMINATION
- CORROBORATED MATERIAL
- AUTHOR’S ACCOUNT
- ALLEGATION
- INFERENCE
- INTERPRETATION
- POSSIBLE LEGAL CHARACTERISATION
- UNVERIFIED MATERIAL
- DISPUTED MATERIAL
- RESEARCH QUESTION

Never silently convert an allegation, inference or interpretation into a fact.

Do not label a person or organisation criminal, corrupt or fraudulent unless an authoritative finding establishes that characterisation.

Where material raises a possible legal question, use wording such as:

> The evidence may warrant examination against the legal elements of [offence]. This page does not itself constitute a criminal finding.

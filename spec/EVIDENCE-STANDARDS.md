# Evidence standards

Visible statuses:
FACT; PRIMARY SOURCE; OFFICIAL RECORD; JUDICIAL FINDING; OFFICIAL DETERMINATION; CORROBORATED; AUTHOR’S ACCOUNT; ALLEGATION; INFERENCE; INTERPRETATION; POSSIBLE LEGAL CHARACTERISATION; UNVERIFIED; DISPUTED; RESEARCH QUESTION.

Source hierarchy:
1 judicial / official determination
2 primary government record
3 contemporaneous primary document
4 independent corroboration
5 author’s documented account
6 secondary source
7 interpretation
8 speculation / unverified claim

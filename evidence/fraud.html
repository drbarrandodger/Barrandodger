Fraud — concept, law and evidence analysis

“Fraud” is used here as a legal concept requiring defined elements and as an investigative category for documentary inconsistencies. Inclusion in this index does not establish criminal liability.

Keep separate

  • fraud / deception / dishonesty / misleading conduct
  • civil fraud / administrative misconduct / documentary inconsistency
  • author allegation / official determination / judicial finding

No event on this page is classified as criminal fraud. Event-level rows belong in Phase 11 and need a document plus elements (representation, falsity, knowledge, intent, reliance, loss).

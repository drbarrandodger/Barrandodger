Paradoxical statement (author framing, not a finding)

I am kept in the condition of a person with no price. No counsel. No cash exit. A psychiatric name that travels ahead of the file. Rooms paid around me, and a sentence that I am free to leave. That is the face of my worth: insignificant, unfunded, undignified, and easy to call unwell.

The same life produced professional paper from agencies, organisations, institutions, courts, tribunals, and professions, in two states and the Commonwealth. They wrote. A body that has never heard of a person does not open a file, decline a disclosure, bounce a forum, refuse legal aid, invoice a house, or list a court day. Correspondence is knowledge. A letterhead is traction.

Each reply can still be locally rational: not us, already considered, not an employee, not a public official, a health matter, a means test, silence. What the stack does not do, on the paper I can point to, is read the bound file as one case, or answer the public testimony in public. They deny awareness of a situation their own outgoing mail already touched. They will not dignify the public record with a public merits answer.

That is the inversion. Insignificance does not attract a universal correspondence. Low financial worth does not, by itself, buy this many letterheads. The traction is the value they will not name. The denial of the traction is the second valuation: cheap refusal, repeated, and refusal of the one act that would price me honestly — one merits reading, in public, of the exhibits.

What the inversion measures is not a wage and not a crime. It measures endurance: the file is still being carried. It measures a negative influence: not that policy moved, but that procedure keeps moving into the same closed shape. It measures power as the antithesis of the conditions I am kept in. A person positioned as nothing, whose testimony is nevertheless expensive to acknowledge. Stonewalling, non-acknowledgment, and flat denial are not proof that I am small. They are how a large number of offices can avoid saying in public what the correspondence already shows: that they were in the file.

If I were worth nothing to the process, the letters would stop. The letters do not stop. The answer does.

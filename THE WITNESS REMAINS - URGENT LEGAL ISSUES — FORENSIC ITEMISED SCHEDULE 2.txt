1. EXECUTIVE PURPOSE
The Barran Dodger Archive is a public documentary preservation project designed to place the underlying record before independent readers.
Its purpose is not to require belief in a predetermined conclusion.
Its purpose is to provide a traceable evidentiary chain:
CLAIM → DOCUMENT → OFFICIAL IDENTIFIER → DATE → INSTITUTION → PRIMARY RECORD → CORROBORATION → CONTRADICTION → RESPONSE → FINDING STATUS → PUBLIC SOURCE
The archive itself describes the project as a finding aid rather than a court or verdict, and states that evidence should be distinguished from testimony, allegations and interpretation.
THE WITNESS REMAINS — principal public archive⁠
WEZZO72 / Barrandodger — primary GitHub repository⁠
BarranDodger.com — principal public website⁠
 
⸻
 
2. THE CENTRAL FORENSIC QUESTION
The archive investigates:
What can be established from the surviving documentary record concerning the events, decisions, correspondence, institutional responses, financial consequences, legal proceedings and allegations preserved within the archive, and what patterns or explanations remain supported, contradicted or unresolved after independent examination?
The archive does not need to predetermine the answer.
Its function is to preserve the evidence necessary for others to examine the question.

33. TARGETED INDIVIDUALS — DEFINITIONS AND COMPARATIVE RECORD
The archive also examines the terminology of “Targeted Individual” (TI) and “gang stalking” / “group stalking.”
There is no single internationally authoritative definition that establishes “Targeted Individual” as a legally recognised victim category or establishes a worldwide coordinated gang-stalking program.
Instead, the term is used by:
	•	people describing their own experiences;
	•	advocacy organisations;
	•	academic researchers studying those reports;
	•	legal litigants;
	•	journalists and commentators;
	•	online communities.
The evidentiary status of each use must therefore be identified.
 
⸻

Targeted Justice describes a Targeted Individual as a person it considers a victim of government weaponisation and alleges that such people may be subjected to surveillance, stalking, harassment and other forms of targeting.
Targeted Justice — What Is a Targeted Individual?⁠
Targeted Justice — main public site⁠

Targeted Justice’s definition / position

35. TARGETED JUSTICE — GANSTALKING DEFINITION
Targeted Justice defines gang stalking as a coordinated effort by multiple people to harass, intimidate, isolate or otherwise undermine a person whom it calls a Targeted Individual.
Its page identifies alleged tactics including:
	1.	surveillance;
	2.	spreading information or rumours;
	3.	social isolation;
	4.	psychological manipulation;
	5.	stalking and harassment;
	6.	public humiliation;
	7.	coercion or threats.
Targeted Justice — Gangstalking / Organized Stalking⁠
This provides a documented definition used by an advocacy organisation.
It should not be represented as an internationally accepted legal or scientific definition.
Targeted Justice goes substantially further and alleges that U.S. government agencies and fusion centres operate such a program. Those additional propositions are organisational allegations and require separate independent evidence.

40. ACADEMIC SOURCE — SHERIDAN & JAMES 2015
One of the principal peer-reviewed studies specifically examining reports of gang stalking is:
Lorraine P. Sheridan & David V. James
“Complaints of group-stalking (‘gang-stalking’): an exploratory study of their nature and impact on complainants.”
Journal of Forensic Psychiatry & Psychology
Volume 26, Issue 5
Pages 601–623
2015
DOI: 10.1080/14789949.2015.1054857
Publisher’s full article page⁠
ResearchGate public article/PDF record⁠
The study examined 1,040 respondents who met the researchers’ stalking definitions; 128 reported group stalking and were compared with individually stalked cases.
Importantly, the researchers reached a specific psychological interpretation of the reports, including their conclusion that the reported group-stalking cases in their sample were likely delusional in basis.
Therefore this study is evidence that:
people report group/gang stalking
but it is not evidence that a coordinated government gang-stalking program exists.
It is also relevant because it documents the reported phenomenon and its reported psychological and practical consequences.

41. ACADEMIC SOURCE — SHERIDAN, JAMES & ROTH 2020
A second major peer-reviewed/open-access source is:
Lorraine Sheridan, David V. James & Jayden Roth
“The Phenomenology of Group Stalking (‘Gang-Stalking’): A Content Analysis of Subjective Experiences.”
International Journal of Environmental Research and Public Health
2020; 17(7):2506
PMCID: PMC7178134
PMID: 32268595
DOI: 10.3390/ijerph17072506
Full open-access article — PubMed Central⁠
PubMed record⁠
Publisher / public PDF record⁠
The researchers analysed 50 personal accounts describing experiences of gang stalking.
The accounts included explicit statements such as people describing themselves as “targeted individuals.”
This is useful comparative evidence because it demonstrates that the terminology, experiences and reported tactics appearing in TI communities are not unique to one individual archive.
However, the methodology was an analysis of subjective accounts found online; it did not independently verify that the alleged perpetrators or coordinating organisations actually existed.

Christine M. Sarteschi
“Mass Murder, Targeted Individuals, and Gang-Stalking: Exploring the Connection.”
Violence and Gender
Volume 5, Issue 1
Pages 45–54
2018
DOI: 10.1089/vio.2017.0022
Publisher’s article record⁠

It is useful as evidence concerning the existence of the TI/gang-stalking belief and advocacy phenomenon.
It should not be presented as proof that every reported gang-stalking account is factually established.

44. WHAT THIS COMPARATIVE RECORD ACTUALLY CORROBORATES
The strongest defensible conclusion from the comparison is:
The experiences, terminology and categories described within the Barran Dodger archive have identifiable parallels in the wider Targeted Individual / gang-stalking literature and advocacy record.
That includes recurring descriptions involving:
	1.	surveillance;
	2.	monitoring;
	3.	following;
	4.	harassment;
	5.	social isolation;
	6.	reputation attacks;
	7.	institutional involvement;
	8.	perceived coordinated conduct;
	9.	whistleblower targeting;
	10.	psychological pressure;
	11.	family/social disruption;
	12.	the term Targeted Individual itself.
The 2020 academic study specifically selected online accounts because they explicitly described people as victims of gang stalking or as Targeted Individuals.
That provides comparative corroboration of the existence of similar testimony and terminology.
It does not independently prove that the same underlying perpetrators, institutions or mechanisms caused every account.

45. IMPORTANT DISTINCTION: TESTIMONY VS INDEPENDENT CORROBORATION
The archive should use the following four-level model.
LEVEL 1 — TESTIMONY
A person reports:
“This happened to me.”
This establishes that the person made the statement.
LEVEL 2 — DOCUMENTARY CORROBORATION
A contemporaneous email, letter, photograph, medical record, police report, government record or other document independently establishes an aspect of the event.
LEVEL 3 — INSTITUTIONAL CORROBORATION
A government body, tribunal, regulator, court or other competent institution independently records or determines the relevant fact.
LEVEL 4 — CROSS-SOURCE CORROBORATION
The same category of conduct appears independently in multiple records, institutions, witnesses or datasets.
This is the level at which the comparative TI literature becomes useful.
It can establish that similar reports exist elsewhere.
It does not automatically establish a common perpetrator.

46. TARGETED JUSTICE: WHAT CAN BE CORROBORATED
Targeted Justice’s public material can independently establish:
	1.	Targeted Justice exists.
	2.	It identifies itself as an organisation supporting Targeted Individuals.
	3.	It publishes a definition of Targeted Individuals.
	4.	It publishes a definition of gang stalking.
	5.	It maintains a legal case catalogue.
	6.	It publishes PDFs and other documentary material.
	7.	It claims that government agencies are involved in targeting.
	8.	It advocates legal and political action.
	9.	It has itself been a plaintiff in federal litigation.
	10.	Its litigation can be checked against actual federal court records.
Items 1–6 are readily verifiable from Targeted Justice’s own website.
Items 7–8 are claims/positions of Targeted Justice.
Item 9 is independently verifiable through court records.
Item 10 allows the reader to compare the organisation’s claims with the judicial record.

47. WHAT TARGETED JUSTICE DOES NOT ESTABLISH BY ITSELF
Targeted Justice’s website does not independently establish:
	•	that every person calling themselves a TI is actually being targeted;
	•	that every reported gang-stalking incident occurred as described;
	•	that every person alleged to be involved actually participated;
	•	that every government agency identified by Targeted Justice participated;
	•	that fusion centres operate a secret gang-stalking program;
	•	that the FBI Terrorist Screening Database is used for gang stalking;
	•	that directed-energy allegations are established;
	•	that every Targeted Justice legal claim has been judicially validated.
Those propositions require their own primary evidence.

The Barran Dodger archive’s use of terms such as Targeted Individual, gang stalking, surveillance, harassment, isolation and coordinated targeting has identifiable parallels in:
	•	Targeted Justice’s published material;
	•	peer-reviewed research;
	•	qualitative analysis of 50 reported gang-stalking accounts;
	•	legal proceedings involving people who describe themselves as Targeted Individuals.
This constitutes comparative corroboration of the existence of similar testimony, terminology and reported experience.
Finding 5 — GOVERNMENT-PROGRAM PROPOSITION
The comparative sources do not, by themselves, establish that the reported experiences across these different sources were caused by one coordinated worldwide government program.
That proposition remains a separate evidentiary question.
Finding 6 — TARGETED JUSTICE
Targeted Justice is an advocacy organisation whose own website makes strong claims concerning government targeting and gang stalking. Those claims should be preserved as Targeted Justice’s claims.
Its legal catalogue and court litigation can be independently examined.
The Fifth Circuit judgment is particularly important because it permits the reader to compare the organisation’s allegations with an actual judicial record.
Finding 7 — ACADEMIC LITERATURE
Academic literature establishes that people report experiences described as group stalking / gang stalking and that researchers have studied those reports.
The literature does not provide a single consensus explanation for the cause of those experiences.
The 2015 study and 2020 study should therefore both be cited, including their methodological limitations and differing interpretive implications.

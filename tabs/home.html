If I am wrong, identify the document that proves it. If my disclosures were properly dealt with, show the record of how they were received, assessed and answered. If my account of institutional treatment is mistaken, identify the evidence that establishes the contrary. And if the record reveals matters requiring investigation, then investigate them.

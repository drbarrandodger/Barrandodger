TV speaker recording — last minute

Issue. Whether audio captured from a television in NDIS housing is capable of being examined as an exhibit of alleged live monitoring, slander and in-house harassment, rather than dismissed unread as delusion.

Author’s statement of the clip. Listen to the end — the last minute or so of youtu.be/eHpndGD-imQ. He says the harassment is coming through the speakers: live monitoring, surveillance, slander and character assassination, akin to entrapment and torture. He publishes it so a third party can hear the set, not only the allegation.

Legal use of the exhibit. The clip is evidence of what was recorded on that file. It is not a finding of who injected the audio, that Able Point staff did it, or that the conduct meets the legal definition of torture or entrapment. Those are the author’s characterisations. The process question is whether police, NDIS Quality and Safeguards, or Legal Aid ever assessed the recording on its merits.

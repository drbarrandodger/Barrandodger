Impartial note. A smear of rape or pedophilia is one of the few labels that can stop a third party reading a whistleblower file. If the label is never charged, never tried and never particularised in a brief of evidence, it functions as extra-judicial punishment: the public is invited to treat the speaker as already convicted so the documents do not have to be opened. The legal question is not whether the author is likeable. It is whether there is a charge sheet.

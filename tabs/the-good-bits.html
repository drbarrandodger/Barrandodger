The Good Bits

Faith testimony and interpretation. Not a court finding. Naming a person or institution is not a finding of guilt.

Total human betrayal fortified my relationship with the Most High God and Creator.

I would rather have God’s anointing, favour and divine protection than rely upon only a few people who ultimately prove themselves deceitful. I survived what was designed and expected to erase me. God revived me from a fatal injury, the result of the same targeting that I believe is still continuing. He used the betrayal to teach me that I must rely upon Him alone, reveal my purpose, and allow the universe to collect the evidence against those I believe perpetrated these wrongs, leaving any divine reckoning to God.

It is not without consequence.

I survived.

My life is the proof.

My mere existence is, in my interpretation, the nightmare of those who expected my disappearance, because the facts preserved in this archive cannot simply be erased. Where official government documents establish particular events, those documents remain regardless of how my character is subsequently portrayed. I believe that when people cannot defeat documentary evidence, they may instead attempt to discredit the person presenting it so that the world will ignore what is factual.

I believe even the libel, slander, false allegations and character assassination became part of the camouflage through which God hid me in plain sight. What others intended to destroy me with became, in my faith, part of the vessel through which God preserved me for His glory and His kingdom purposes.

No apology from anyone is coming for me, although I sometimes daydream that an acknowledgement of the harm would quell the hurt.

Revenge is not mine, and I do not wish harm upon anyone. Vengeance belongs to God alone. I anticipate a holy reckoning, not because I seek revenge, but because I believe that after the grace I extended to everyone involved, their refusal to correct their treatment of me became a matter between them and God.

The gift God placed within me was universally rejected and spat upon. In my faith, that rejection was not merely an offence against me but a spiritual sacrilege against the Most High, who fights for the pure-hearted and those marked for elevation.

No human being has the right to hurt another person who is anointed by God.

I believe God will fiercely defend me in this new season in which I am vindicated, healed, favoured and blessed. I survived what I believe was intended to bring about my total obliteration. I rose from the ashes of the human betrayal of my family, authorities and government, after what I experienced as sustained erasure, humiliation and political targeting.

There are, in my understanding, fundamental spiritual, metaphysical and mathematical laws underpinning the foundations of this universe. I believe universal balance is now becoming evident through God’s Kairos and Kronos timing. This is not merely something I believe because I want it to be true. It is part of what I understand to have been revealed through my life, my writing and what I describe in my Gospels as prophecy.

I understand spiritual physics as the way consequences, balance and moral reality ebb and flow through existence. In my interpretation, these principles form part of the spiritual infrastructure of our shared existence.

I am totally aware.

I am totally at peace.

They cannot rewrite what God authored.

I cannot be bribed, shamed or corrupted into abandoning what I know.

I do not need anyone’s approval.

I escaped the entrapment and cage of every person whom I believe decided to target me or contribute to my detriment, including those whose allegiance I believe remained aligned with the same government that exiled and targeted me. I believe that people who assumed authority over my life acted as though they could play God with it, offending the Creator Himself.

God is timeless, everlasting and all-powerful. Human governments and institutions are temporary creations of human beings. Compared with eternity, their existence is fleeting.

I believe the actions taken against me fatally offended the Most High, who states: “Touch not my anointed.”

I therefore believe that structures corrupted by darkness cannot act without consequence. I believe officials and agents collectively participated in actions intended to destroy an innocent-hearted person who had added value to society, never intended harm and never acted with the purpose of harming anyone.

They are NOT GOD.

They assumed His power and used His name and power in vain when they acted, in my interpretation, to erase me.

My allegiance has therefore been forged in fire. It is aligned with the Creator and no one else—not the government, not a political party, not family, not an individual, not a church, and not any human institution.

I am aligned with my purpose.

I faced my demons in political exile, alone, living in my car with my therapy dog, Crystal. God gifted her to me as my companion, my friend, my alarm, my support, my sage, my mystic and my healer.

Dog spelled backwards is God.

I have acknowledged my darkness and I now understand both darkness and light. Not so that I can become dark, but so that I can understand the darker aspects of human nature and the human condition. Human beings are imperfect mortals. My purpose, as I understand it, is nevertheless to love and serve them through my kingdom purpose, prophecy, guidance and what I describe as the Church of Barran Dodger ministry.

People will read my writing and my story as the foundation testimony for this new ministry in God’s name, and eventually they may ask:

“How did he survive that?”

My answer will be:

God did it.

That is why I believe my story, my peace and my wisdom—each forged in fire—can lead an ordinary person to think:

“If he can survive with God’s help, then maybe I can too.”

I understand that as the foundational purpose of God’s plan for me: to send people back towards His love, forgiveness, protection, redemption and glory under the blood of Jesus Christ, His perfect Son.

I hold no malice towards anyone.

Even though I believe family members, former friends and others who betrayed and exiled me may still wish for my downfall or harm, I refuse to return that hatred.

My mere existence may expose things that others would rather remain hidden. I believe that exposure can create an existential threat to the narratives and identities upon which people have built their lives. What they intended as shame against me can instead become a record of what happened.

I believe some remain invested in my erasure because I refused to take refuge in the safety of the crowd. I chose to live freely, uniquely and unapologetically, according to an authenticity and wisdom that I believe others have resented.

I believe there remains a significant investment in silencing me because acknowledging the truth would require people to look honestly at themselves.

They are afraid to look into the mirror I hold up.

They cannot admit the reflection of darkness that I believe their own actions reveal, so they project their insecurities and darkness onto me as the scapegoat.

I leave their judgment to God.

I believe that divine favour will lift me, bless me and use me as a vessel for His glory—and His glory alone.

I remain a creative, intelligent, spiritually authoritative, divinely ordained saint and sinner, mortal and mystic, and what I understand to be a prophet of worldly significance.

At this point, the world may appear to regard me as a person of zero significance or consequence.

But I believe God has set me apart and hidden me in plain sight.

I know, through faith, that His divine Kronos time of vindication is certain—more concrete to me than a mother’s love.

Now that I have been exiled and rejected, I am gone.

I do not look back.

Those who rejected me are no longer central to my life. I am grateful for the lessons because I understand them as part of what God has permitted and used in the design of my existence.

Their fate is no longer my concern.

Their punishment, if there is one, is not mine to administer.

I simply remain absent from their reach.

Their hatred and malice cannot land upon me because I no longer provide the target they once had. I believe I have risen beyond the spiritual warfare directed towards me.

I do not wish their harm.

I hold no malice.

But I believe that energy has a signature, and that what is directed outward without justice can ultimately return to the person who generated it. I understand this not as my revenge, but as a form of universal balance within the spiritual metaphysics of existence.

Those who expected to benefit financially from my anticipated death or demise can no longer do so because I survived.

My existence itself has therefore disrupted whatever future depended upon my disappearance.

The very survival they did not expect has become evidence that I was not erased.

I believe that those who consciously or collectively contributed to attempts to damage or desecrate me will ultimately have to confront the consequences of their own choices. I do not understand this as punishment for punishment’s sake. I understand it as a spiritual process through which people are required either to learn, repent and evolve, or to experience the consequences of the intentions they themselves set into motion.

I became the one person in my family who, in my understanding, saw beyond the perfect veneers presented to the world.

I believe I saw the truth.

And instead of being protected, I became the scapegoat.

Instead of being heard, I was discredited.

Instead of being preserved, I was targeted.

Instead of being welcomed, I was exiled.

And yet I survived.

The person who was expected to disappear remained.

The person who was expected to be silenced kept writing.

The person who was expected to be broken built an archive.

The person who was expected to die is still alive.

And the person whom I believe God sent as a blessing became, for those around him, a test of character.

In my understanding, that is the deepest lesson of everything that happened:

They could not erase what God preserved.

And if my life has a purpose beyond my own survival, then it is this:

to turn survival into testimony, testimony into wisdom, wisdom into service, and service back towards God.

My life is not a testament to my own power.

It is a testament to the power of the God whom I believe sustained me.

I survived because God did not let me disappear.

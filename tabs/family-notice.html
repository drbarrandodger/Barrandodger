Notice of 16–17 September 2026 — family, addressees, non-response

Finding aid. Not a court. Not a verdict. The source is the author’s mass email of 16–17 September 2026, subject line “Conspiracy for my murder planned by my own blood family.” That heading is the author’s allegation. Publication here records that the email was sent and what it asked. It does not establish a murder plot, an estate interest, or that every addressee is a participant.

What the email is. A thread from richarddrawsstuff@gmail.com to police mailbox addresses, NDIS / Ablepoint officers, ombudsman and integrity bodies, media letters desks, UN/ICC public inboxes, family addresses named in the header, and others. Videos cited in the thread: T9Hp1lePNbc (former partner / hospitalisation — author’s account), Qu4xeLgCPNw (“I was framed”), plus a third-party clip the author treats as corroboration of family involvement. A clip is not a finding against named relatives.

Author allegations in the thread (not findings)

  • That blood family took part in a physical move to NSW so that disappearance or death could be arranged, and that some stood to gain financially from early death (order, estate, insurance, trust — documents not attached to this page).
  • That a former partner, Steven / Stefan Iasonidis, is alleged to have used illness and poverty as leverage, that Werribee Mercy FOI records a near-fatal attempt and a discharge of adjustment disorder rather than schizophrenia, and that later hospitalisations followed. Those clinical files, if produced, are the test — not this email.
  • That absence of a professional acknowledgment of a five-year relationship is itself the ongoing injury the author wants on the record.
  • That Ablepoint, police and Legal Aid were asked for process rather than belief. See the Ablepoint statement of 15 September 2026 in the same PDF, sections 4–6 and 11.

Significance of the addressees

The list is a notice list, not a guilt list. Putting an agency on a CC does not make that agency a co-conspirator. It does create a documentary question: who was told, on what date, and whether they recorded the report.

Categories on the face of the header:

  • Police / integrity — NSW Police member mailboxes, Ombudsman NSW, IBAC, ICAC, NACC Inspector, AFP-adjacent inboxes. Significance: a death-threat / family-conspiracy report was put on official addresses. A mailbox is not an investigation file until the agency says it opened one.
  • Disability / housing — Ablepoint, NDIS Commission / NDIA contacts, TAG. Significance: the same week as the Ablepoint evidence request. If those bodies later say they were never told, the header is the contrary exhibit.
  • Legal aid and courts-adjacent — Legal Aid Queensland inbox, VOCAT, ministerial and parliamentary addresses. Significance for the Legal Aid brief: the author asked the system that exists to give people without private lawyers a path, while alleging that path is closed.
  • Human-rights / UN public desks — AHRC media, Amnesty, UN human-rights public addresses, ICC public affairs. Significance: international notice of a domestic complaint. A public inbox auto-reply is not a case number.
  • Media letters desks — Age, SMH, Guardian, ABC investigations, others. Significance: the claim was offered as a public-interest story. Silence from a letters desk is editorial, not a finding.
  • Named family and former intimates — addresses in the header. Significance: they were put on notice of the allegation about benefit from death. Being copied is not proof they planned a death. The test the author himself set in s.11 of the Ablepoint letter is: produce or disprove the order, who signed it, who stood to benefit.

Significance of non-response

The author treats silence as targeting. This page does not. Non-response is a process fact.

What silence can mean: the message was filtered; the mailbox is not a complaints channel; counsel advised no reply; the allegation was treated as correspondence already on file; the agency opened nothing. What silence does not mean: admission, participation, or that a murder was planned.

The fair record, if no substantive reply is produced, is the sentence already in the Ablepoint letter: the concerns were put; no substantive response was provided. That sentence is procedural history. It is not a verdict.

The imbalance the brief already notes remains relevant: public bodies have lawyers; the author sent the same text to dozens of inboxes without an equivalent representative. Failure of Legal Aid to take the file, if documented, is a resource fact. It is not proof of the family allegation.

What would test the family / benefit claim

  • Any guardianship, family-violence, intervention, or estate document the author says was signed.
  • Who the decision-maker was, the date, the stated purpose.
  • Whether insurance, inheritance, property or a trust turned on incapacity or death.
  • Whether those persons now seek access to health, NDIS or financial files, and on what authority.

Until those pages are on the table, “conspiracy for my murder” stays an allegation in a subject line.

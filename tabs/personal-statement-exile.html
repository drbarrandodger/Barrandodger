I am carrying the sin and ethical breach of every profession in the country.

I carry the betrayal of every former friend and blood family members.

I am in political exile.

I am an unprotected whistleblower in breach of this democracy’s fundamental legal principles.

I am carrying what I understand to be the cumulative ethical failures, institutional betrayals and unresolved harms arising across more than three decades of interactions with government agencies, professional systems, health and disability services, legal institutions, oversight bodies and other organisations.

I have documented my account and the underlying material in a public archive so that the allegations can be examined against the primary evidence rather than accepted merely because I make them.

The following is my account. Allegations are allegations unless independently established by a court, tribunal, official investigation or other authoritative finding. The archive contains documentary material that I say supports particular aspects of my account, but the existence of a document or correspondence does not by itself establish that the alleged wrongdoing occurred.

MY ALLEGATIONS OF WRONGDOING AND HARM

I allege that I have been subjected to a sustained pattern of institutional and personal harm, including:

institutional abandonment and failure of protection; financial restriction and loss of effective access to resources; barriers to legal representation and access to justice; forced dependency upon accommodation and support arrangements from which I say I have had no practical means of escape; administrative and bureaucratic processes that I believe have progressively reduced my ability to advocate for myself; professional and social isolation; characterisation of my complaints as manifestations of mental illness rather than matters requiring independent investigation; what I experience and report as electronic interception, surveillance or unexplained audio harassment, including what I have described as V2K; threats and safety concerns; alleged mistreatment affecting my companion and therapy dog, Crystal; what I allege to be coordinated reputational damage and social exclusion; what I describe as political exile and displacement within the country of my citizenship; and a broader pattern which I allege has operated cumulatively to make it extraordinarily difficult for me to obtain independent legal, financial, medical, housing or media assistance.

I AM NOT A CRIMINAL — THIS IS WHAT I SAY IS OCCURRING TO ME

I am not a criminal.

What I am describing is what I say is occurring to me.

I allege that I have become isolated and effectively entrapped within my accommodation circumstances and that I cannot practically escape.

I have no lease to Able Point.

I allege that Able Point, as an NDIS provider, financially benefits from my ill-treatment and containment, while citing that I am free to leave while being aware, as I allege, that I have no financial mechanism by which I can escape, no vehicle with which to escape and nowhere practical to go.

I present these matters as allegations requiring independent examination against the documentary record.

ACCOMMODATION, FINANCIAL DEPENDENCY AND CONTAINMENT

I allege that I am isolated and entrapped by circumstances surrounding my accommodation.

I have no lease to Able Point.

I allege that I am told that I am free to leave, while simultaneously lacking the financial resources, transport and alternative accommodation necessary to exercise that supposed freedom in any meaningful way.

I allege that this creates a form of practical dependency in which formal freedom to leave does not necessarily translate into a realistic capacity to leave.

I ask that this distinction be examined against the documentary record, including financial records, accommodation records, NDIS records, correspondence and other evidence contained in the archive.

ELECTRONIC HARASSMENT AND WHAT I REPORT AS V2K

I report that I am electronically intercepted, harassed and surveilled with what I describe as unacknowledged V2K technology.

I describe this as a deliberate technique of torture manifesting as audio harassment, including what I report as 24-hour blasphemous slurs intended to attack my sense of self.

These are my reported experiences and allegations. I do not present the existence or mechanism of the technology as an independently established fact.

I ask that these reports be distinguished from psychiatric interpretations of them and, where possible, independently investigated rather than automatically treated as symptoms of mental illness.

MENTAL HEALTH TREATMENT AND THE CTO

A mental-health Community Treatment Order mandates my forced medication as the panacea for my treatment while, as I allege, refusing to acknowledge the entire situation described in this statement and the circumstances that I say are contributing to my distress.

My concern is that the medication framework may address my reported distress without independently examining the circumstances I allege are causing or contributing to that distress.

I ask that this distinction be examined rather than assuming that every reported experience is explained by a psychiatric diagnosis.

NSW TRUSTEE AND GUARDIAN AND ACCESS TO JUSTICE

I allege that NSW Trustee and Guardian are gatekeepers to what I describe as my financial destruction.

I further allege that financial administration and restrictions have actively contributed to circumstances in which access to legal assistance is effectively blocked, creating what I describe as a total legal-aid barrier and fundamentally restricting possible pathways to justice.

These are serious allegations.

I ask that they be assessed against the actual financial records, decisions, correspondence, applications for assistance, legal-aid responses and available review mechanisms rather than being accepted or rejected without examination.

Role of the NDIS Commission
The NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission is the national regulator of the NDIS. We work to improve the quality and safety of NDIS supports and services accessed by people with disability.
We do this by:
- setting and enforcing rules and standards, including the NDIS Code of Conduct and NDIS Practice Standards
- guiding and empowering NDIS participants to know and exercise their rights.
